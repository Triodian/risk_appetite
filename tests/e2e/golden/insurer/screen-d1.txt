Decision 1 · session 1
Authority

How much authority may AI hold in each function, whether it is used by our own staff, by a provider, or by a platform the provider relies on?

Fails checks
Answered as: A ceiling, A1 to A6, for each function and each of the three locations
Purpose of this decision
The board’s directions for this decision · BD1, BD2, BD3

From AI Board Direction, Corvane General Insurance · version V2, recorded by the chair. A position that goes past a direction cannot form part of the working appetite until a reason and a board revisit are recorded (check K11).

BD1 Posture by area

Underwriting and pricing: Cautious · up to A2 · Within the rating guide; an underwriter approves any change to a rate
Claims: Cautious · up to A2 · A person decides every decline
Fraud and investigations: Cautious · up to A2 · An investigator decides whether a claim is treated as suspected fraud
Policy administration: Open · up to A4 · Within the rules the chief underwriting officer has approved; anything outside them goes to a person
Complaints and disputes: Cautious · up to A2
Customer communications: Open · up to A4 · General information only; anything about a customer’s own claim or cover goes to a person
Finance and reserving: Cautious · up to A2
Legal and compliance: Avoid · up to A1
Technology operations: Open · up to A4 · No AI holds credentials to production systems unattended

BD2 Red lines

AI declining a claim with no person reviewing the decision (Claims)
AI deciding a claimant is fraudulent with no person reviewing it (Fraud and investigations)
AI setting a premium outside the rating guide with no underwriter approving it (Underwriting and pricing)
Board, committee or transaction information (G6) entered into any tool outside the insurer’s own estate (All areas)

BD3 Reserved decisions

A new AI use at A3 (Decides) or above · Before it starts, brought by CEO
A new AI use in an area marked Avoid · Before it starts, brought by CEO
An exception to a red line · Before it starts, brought by CEO
A claims manager or an underwriting agency changing the AI it uses on customers’ claims or premiums · Before it starts, where the agreement allows; otherwise told at the next meeting, brought by CRO
A material AI incident · Told within 24 hours, brought by CEO
A change to the directions or the measures · Before it takes effect, brought by Chair
