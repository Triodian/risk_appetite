## The eight positions

- **Underwriting and pricing.** Own staff: up to A2 Recommends. Inside a provider: up to A3 Decides. Platform behind a provider: up to A2 Recommends.
- **Claims.** Own staff: up to A3 Decides. Inside a provider: up to A4 Moves money (Up to $5,000 per claim; above that a person approves). Platform behind a provider: up to A3 Decides.
- **Fraud and investigations.** Own staff: up to A2 Recommends. Inside a provider: up to A3 Decides. Platform behind a provider: up to A2 Recommends.
- **Policy administration.** Own staff: up to A4 Moves money. Inside a provider: up to A4 Moves money. Platform behind a provider: up to A3 Decides.
- **Complaints and disputes.** Own staff: up to A2 Recommends. Inside a provider: up to A2 Recommends. Platform behind a provider: up to A1 Assists.
- **Customer communications.** Own staff: up to A3 Decides (Templates approved by Compliance). Inside a provider: up to A3 Decides. Platform behind a provider: up to A2 Recommends.
- **Finance and reserving.** Own staff: up to A2 Recommends. Inside a provider: up to A2 Recommends. Platform behind a provider: up to A1 Assists.
- **Legal and compliance.** Own staff: up to A1 Assists. Inside a provider: up to A1 Assists. Platform behind a provider: up to A1 Assists.
- **Technology operations.** Own staff: up to A4 Moves money. Inside a provider: up to A4 Moves money. Platform behind a provider: up to A3 Decides.

### 2. Consequence thresholds

When a failure counts as each level of consequence.

- **C2 Contract breach:** Breach of a material outsourcing term
- **C3 Financial loss:** $250,000 single event; $1m in a quarter
- **C4 Policyholder harm:** 10 or more policyholders affected, or any single case of serious financial hardship
- **C5 Critical disruption · Claims payment:** No more than 2 business days without the ability to pay claims
- **C5 Critical disruption · Policy issuance and renewal:** No more than 3 business days
- **C5 Critical disruption · Premium collection:** No more than 5 business days

### 4. Shortfall tolerance

When the evidence falls short, how serious the shortfall is and how long it may continue.

- **High priority:** Reported to the next board risk committee meeting, with a plan. While the gap stands: One of: restrict the AI's authority; add a compensating control; or accept, with a named accountable person.
- **Medium priority:** Closed by the next contract renewal or variation for the arrangement. While the gap stands: Accept, recorded with an owner.
- **Low priority:** Reviewed at the next annual review. While the gap stands: Accept.

### 5. Unknowns

How much Corvane General Insurance (fictional) may not know about AI in its supply chain.

- **C1 Immaterial or C2 Contract breach:** not-known facts allowed per entry: No limit. How long an F4 finding may stand: Until the next annual review. Minimum confidence: Low.
- **C3 Financial loss:** not-known facts allowed per entry: 2. How long an F4 finding may stand: Until the next contract renewal or variation. Minimum confidence: Medium.
- **C4 Policyholder harm or C5 Critical disruption:** not-known facts allowed per entry: None among the facts that determine the authority level. How long an F4 finding may stand: 30 June 2027; then the provider is asked. Minimum confidence: Medium.

### 8. Change and review

Review interval: Annual, in the same cycle as the main risk appetite statement. The appetite, or an arrangement, comes back before then when:

- A new arrangement where AI is at A3 Decides or above: That arrangement is assessed before it starts
- Trigger TR1, TR6, TR7 or TR8 occurs: The register entry is reassessed and its finding set again
- An arrangement reaches renewal or variation of its contract: Its evidence terms are reviewed against the board's expectation
- A material AI incident, at the insurer or in its supply chain: Decisions 1, 3 and 7
- New regulatory guidance on AI or outsourcing: All eight decisions, on counsel's advice
- The review interval set by the board has passed: All eight decisions
