# Attachment A. AI risk appetite statement

## 1. Purpose and scope

This statement covers AI used by Corvane General Insurance (fictional), by its material service providers, and by the platforms behind them. It sits within Corvane General Insurance (fictional)'s board-approved risk appetite statement, and uses the loss thresholds and critical operation tolerance levels the board has already approved.

## Appendix. Definitions

Definitions from the AI Use Assessment Framework, sections 8.1 to 8.3.

| Code | Authority tier | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 Assists | productivity | Drafts, summarises, searches. A person still acts. |
| A2 Recommends | analytical | Scores, ranks, recommends. A person decides. |
| A3 Decides | decision | Approves, declines, triages. The output is the decision; no person reviews before it takes effect. |
| A4 Moves money | transactional | Prices, allocates, pays. Money or positions move. |
| A5 Acts unattended | agentic | Plans and acts across steps and tools, unattended. |
| A6 Changes systems | actuation | Holds credentials that change systems, assets or infrastructure. |

| Code | Consequence level | Generic definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 Immaterial | immaterial | No effect outside the team using it. |
| C2 Contract breach | contract breach | The provider or insurer would be in breach of an agreement term. |
| C3 Financial loss | financial loss | Loss to the insurer above its reporting threshold for the operation. |
| C4 Policyholder harm | policyholder harm | A policyholder is wrongly declined, delayed, charged or paid. |
| C5 Critical disruption | critical-operation disruption | A critical operation moves outside the tolerance the board has set. |

| Code | Evidence rung | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E0 None | none | Nothing is held. |
| E1 Disclosure | disclosure | A statement of what AI is used, where and for what. |
| E2 Attestation | attestation | A signed statement that the AI operated within stated limits over a period. |
| E3 Event notice | event notification | Notice when a defined trigger is crossed. Also called trigger reporting. |
| E4 Audit right | audit | A right to examine the aggregate record, exercised by the insurer or its verifier. |
| E5 Live telemetry | continuous telemetry | A stream of aggregate measures against the declared appetite. Also called continuous evidence. |
| E6 Action control | deterministic actuation control | A control at the point of action, independent of the AI's reasoning, with a signed record of what was requested, what applied and what was allowed. |

# Attachment D. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Above ceiling | An AI use with more authority than the ceiling the board has set. It is reported separately from evidence shortfalls. |
| Accountable person | The senior person responsible for a function under the Financial Accountability Regime. |
| AI risk appetite | The board's statement of how much authority AI may hold, what failures it will tolerate, and what evidence it expects the insurer to hold. |
| Authority tier | How much AI may do without a person deciding, from A1 Assists to A6 Changes systems. |
| Calibration set | The eleven parameters, P1 to P11, that the AI Use Assessment reads. |
| Ceiling | The highest authority tier the board accepts for a function and location. |
| Concentration | The share of critical operations that depend on a single platform behind a provider or AI model provider. |
| Condition | A limit attached to a ceiling, such as a maximum amount. It must be capable of being checked against documents. |
| Confidence | How firmly the obtainable evidence is established: high if seen, medium if described, and low if inferred. |
| Consequence level | How serious a failure would be, from C1 Immaterial to C5 Critical disruption (disruption of a critical operation). |
| Consistency check | One of ten tests, K1 to K10, applied to the recorded positions. |
| Counsel | The law firm leading the engagement. Counsel is responsible for every legal judgement. |
| Critical operation | A business process whose disruption the insurer has assessed as critical, with a tolerance level approved by the board. |
| Current state | What the insurer does or holds today. It is the starting point for decisions 1 and 3. |
| Evidence ladder | The seven evidence levels, in order of strength. |
| Evidence rung | A level of evidence, from E0 None to E6 Action control. |
| Expected evidence | The evidence the board expects the insurer to hold for a given authority tier and consequence level. |
| Gap | The number of evidence levels between required and obtainable evidence. |
| Gap priority | How serious a gap is, based on its size and the consequence level. |
| Interim position | What the insurer does while a gap remains open: restrict the AI's authority, add a compensating control, or accept the gap. |
| Material service provider | A provider the insurer relies on for a critical operation or to manage a material risk. |
| Not determinable (F4) | An assessment finding that the documents cannot show whether, or how, AI is used. |
| Not known | A fact the insurer's documents do not show. Such facts are counted, not estimated. |
| Obtainable evidence | The evidence that can actually be produced and independently checked. |
| Platform behind a provider | The technology, pricing service or AI model a provider relies on (a fourth party). |
| Proposer | The person who put a position forward. On decisions 1 and 3 this must be the CRO or the insurer's risk function. |
| Required evidence | The evidence the insurer can reasonably require from a provider under its contract, as settled with counsel. |
| Risk appetite statement | The board-approved statement of the risk the insurer is prepared to accept. The AI risk appetite sits within it. |
| Role limits | Administrators complete set-up. Counsel, Triodian, the CRO and the insurer's risk function record positions and starting data. The independent reviewer may change decision 2 only, including its starting data, questions and challenge. The board record role may complete the approval fields only. Only counsel or the CRO may write the overall position. Challenge responses and their status are recorded by counsel, the CRO or the risk function. |
| Starting position | A value filled in before the session as a basis for discussion. It is not a recommendation. |
| Tolerance level | The maximum disruption to a critical operation the board accepts. |
| Trigger | A defined event that must be reported to the insurer within a set time. |
| Who may propose | On decisions 1 and 3, only the CRO or the insurer's risk function may propose a position. On decision 2, the independent reviewer may also propose. Counsel and Triodian explain and record positions; neither proposes them. |
| Working appetite | Positions agreed by the CRO and used to calibrate the assessment before the board approves them. |
